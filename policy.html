Policy Formulation

Decentralizing education is a big step in my plan. Having a single body of government figuring out a single catch-all system for districts that are distinct, culturally different, and of varying backgrounds is an impossibility that has crippled our education for far too long. Local schools, parents of the students, and faculty should have a much better say in how the school should opperate, as they are the most qualified to understand the needs of the students, which should always be the top priority

Teacher Training

Nobody understands what the students need more than the teacher. Under our current system, we have extremely well trained and proficient teachers, but they're being pigeonheld into a curriculum that they have very little control over. Having requirements that each student needs to learn is a good thing, but the teacher should be encouraged to explore and test new methods of teaching the material, in a way that they can cater towards their students. Lectures should take a backseat as it is a quick way to get students disinterested in a subject, and hands on experiences and group ativities should be put to the forefront

Student Support

A school should have an approachable and diverse support system for students. The support faculty should have an understandment of the culture of the district. Diversity among the support is extremely important, so that all students have someone that they feel like will understand their struggles and concerns, that they're being heard. Staff should be able to assist students and parents in choosing the correct education path for them, and assist in getting financial support for those that need it.

Competition and Choice

Competition between schools leads to schools improving the quality of their education. Parents want the best education for their kids and when quality education is the standard, the only factor becomes locale. With how I want to propose funding, school funding is partly supplied by the number of students, so schools are incentivised to attract as many students as their capacity allows for.
